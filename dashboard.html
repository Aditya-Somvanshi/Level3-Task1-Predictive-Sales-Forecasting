Historical pattern summary

Complete months only (Jan 2024 to Apr 2025).

Method. Six models were fitted to 16 complete months: naive, 3-month moving average, average level, exponential smoothing, linear trend and damped Holt trend. Each was scored with a rolling-origin backtest (train on the first 8 to 15 months, forecast up to 3 months ahead). Prediction intervals use the backtest error of the chosen model and widen after 3 months because the backtest cannot measure further out.

Limits. With 16 months of data, seasonality (such as a possible December lift) cannot be confirmed, and the forecast assumes conditions stay similar. Treat the numbers as a baseline range, not a promise. May 2025 is excluded from model fitting because the data stops on 15 May; its run rate is shown only as a cross-check.
